01

Fraud intelligence · Production ML

NovaPay FraudGuard

A real-time fraud detection system built for high-stakes payment decisions. I trained and tuned a LightGBM classifier on 11,140 transactions, used chronological validation to prevent future leakage, and designed the workflow around the precision–recall trade-off that matters in imbalanced fraud data.

The selected model reached 0.962 PR-AUC with 99.6% fraud precision and 91.6% recall. SHAP makes each prediction explainable, while FastAPI, Docker and a Streamlit interface turn the model into a product that investigators can actually use.

Financial stability · Predictive analytics

BoaBank AI

An early-warning platform for financial risk across West Africa. The system combines World Bank and IMF indicators into transparent Financial Risk and Banking Resilience indices, then tracks how inflation, growth, currency pressure and private-credit conditions change over time.

The 2004–2025 panel supports next-year risk prediction, country comparison and policy-ready diagnostics. I paired the modelling layer with a FastAPI service and Streamlit dashboard so analysts can move from a regional signal to a clear supervisory recommendation.

Public health · Early-warning system

Tanara AI

A public-health early-warning system built for ten ASEAN countries. I engineered Health Risk and Readiness indices from development and health indicators, trained interpretable models to estimate next-year risk, and created a priority matrix that separates urgent threats from longer-term capacity gaps.

The finished product connects prediction with action: interactive country profiles, regional comparisons and practical intervention recommendations. The application is deployed on AWS infrastructure with a Streamlit decision interface.

03 / About

From evidence to impact.

Hi, I’m Uchechukwu—a Data Scientist with 3+ years of experience building decision-ready systems across finance, policy and operational analytics.

I’m completing a Master’s in Economics at Sciences Po, with a strong focus on data science, AI and machine learning. Alongside my studies, I develop real-world products that combine statistical modelling, machine learning and data engineering.

At Agora Policy, I automated data-validation workflows for millions of financial and economic records from sources including Nigeria’s National Bureau of Statistics, the World Bank and the IMF. I also created 250+ publication-ready visualisations and supported policy research, economic analysis and data-driven reporting.

At 10Alytics, I built and compared classification, regression and forecasting models with Scikit-learn, ARIMA and Prophet, then communicated the results through Power BI, Tableau and Python-based visualisations.

I specialise in end-to-end data products: statistical models and ML pipelines with Scikit-learn and MLflow; ETL systems using Airflow, dbt and Snowflake; and deployable analytics powered by FastAPI, Streamlit and cloud infrastructure. My goal is to move work beyond the notebook into systems people can use.
